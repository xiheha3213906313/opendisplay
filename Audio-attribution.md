# Android system audio attribution

- The AUD1 framing and Android AudioTrack player are adapted from
  [gprot42/android-opendisplay at 6f568c1](https://github.com/gprot42/android-opendisplay/tree/6f568c136e8b028accd2955855dd5817cd2c8eb6)
  under the [GNU GPL v3](LICENSE).
- The HAL plug-in interface, clock model, and output stream properties
  are adapted from Apple's
  [Creating an Audio Server Driver Plug-in sample](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/coreaudio/creating-an-audio-server-driver-plug-in).
  Its [license notice](MacAudioDriver/APPLE-LICENSE.txt) is included.
- The mixed stereo PCM ring is adapted from
  [BlackHole at 6008d29](https://github.com/ExistentialAudio/BlackHole/blob/6008d295ff00c8b57412a23564619963f4ecbeac/BlackHole/BlackHole.c)
  under the [GNU GPL v3](LICENSE). Copyright © 2019 Existential Audio Inc.
